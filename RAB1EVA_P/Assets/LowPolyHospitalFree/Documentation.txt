Low Poly Hospital Free

Documentation

Every prop is a prefab in Assets/LowPolyHospitalFree/Prefabs/: drag it into a scene. 1 unit = 1 m; a prop's pivot
is on the floor (or, for a wall prop, on the wall) it rests against. Each part has a box collider.

Look
Every prefab uses Materials/, on the shader LowPolyHospitalFree/Stylized Lit: the texture times the occlusion baked into the
vertex colours (Baked Occlusion), lit by the scene's lights with a soft falloff (Light Wrap), casting and
taking shadows, with fog; Glow Map's texels light themselves (lamps, screens, signs; Glow Strength).
Render pipelines: Built-in and URP: every material uses the pack's own lit shader, with a SubShader for each. Not HDRP, where the materials show magenta until given an HDRP Lit shader.

Animations
A prop with moving parts has an Animation component with its clips. Standalone decorative loops start automatically.
Clips controlled by use, pickup, gun or melee behavior wait for that control. Play a clip directly with:
    GetComponent<Animation>().Play("open");
Demo/Demo.unity circles the props and plays every animation in turn (Scripts/PropsDemo.cs).

Machines
A prop that works (its kind is in the list below) has Scripts/Machine.cs on its prefab, set up in the
inspector: its kind (what a use does: Switch, Button, Turn, Slot, Keypad, Panel, Item, Powered, Gauge),
the machines it needs to be powered, and UnityEvents for what it does (onSwitched, onLive, onRefused,
onUsed, onKeyed, onPicked). Machine.cs's comments say what each kind does.
    lever.Use(carried);                     // the player's use; carried: the items they hold
    keypad.Use(carried, "keypad_key_4");    // a key: the part they point at
    door.needs = new[] { lever };           // the door is powered while the lever is on

Textures: atlas.png 2048x2048, atlas_glow.png 2048x2048, floor_hosp.png 128x128, floor_ward.png 128x128, wall_brick.png 128x128, wall_hosp.png 128x128, wall_hosp_dado.png 128x128, filtered with mipmaps, colour only: the shading is baked into vertex colours, so no normal maps.
